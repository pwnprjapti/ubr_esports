## 📜 Key Clauses & Agreement Terms

### 1. Parties & Purpose
- **Client:** UBR Esports Team (info@ubresports.com)
- **Developer:** Development & Tech Team (dev@ubresports.com)
- **Purpose:** Development and deployment of the UBR Esports Tournament & Virtual Wallet Platform.

### 2. Scope of Services
Developer shall deliver a functional web application with:
- Google OAuth User Login system.
- Tournament/Match booking and slot limits logic.
- Virtual Wallet ledger supporting cash deposits (screenshot audits) and withdrawals (UPI/QR code dispatch).
- Full Admin Control Console & live Leaderboards.

### 3. Financial Terms
- **Total Project Fee:** INR 57,000 (Fifty-Seven Thousand Rupees).
- **Payment Schedule:**
  - **40% (INR 22,800) Advance:** Due upon contract signing.
  - **40% (INR 22,800) Milestone:** Due upon booking and wallet completion.
  - **20% (INR 11,400) Retainage:** Due upon final deploy and testing handoff.

### 4. Intellectual Property (IP)
- Ownership of custom source code, configurations, database models, and layout designs will be assigned exclusively to the **Client** upon final receipt of the total project fee.
- Reusable framework parts and proprietary core libraries remain the property of the **Developer**.

### 5. Confidentiality & Credentials
- Developer shall maintain strict confidentiality regarding all Client tournament, user, and transaction data.
- Developer shall protect database connection strings and Cloudinary secret keys using standard `.env` protection.

### 6. Support & Liability Limits
- **Complimentary Support:** 30 Days of bug-fixing support starts immediately from live deployment.
- **Liability Cap:** Developer's liability under this agreement is capped at the total amount paid by the Client.
